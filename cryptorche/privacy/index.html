Cryptorche: privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Version française

1. Controller

Marega Studio (Fodié Marega, registration in progress). Contact: lufygate@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

  • An anonymous account identifier and a device secret, generated by the game. No name or e-mail address is requested. They are stored encrypted on your device.
  • Match summaries (floor reached, duration, win or loss, rewards) and your progress: balances (shards, gems), quests, season pass, inventory.
  • On Android, for purchases: the purchase token and product identifier, sent to Google Play for verification.
  • Your IP address, processed transiently to limit abuse (rate limiting). It is not kept in our logs.
  • If you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad (from version 1.0): Google AdMob processes your device advertising ID and technical data, with your consent (see section 4).

We never collect your name, e-mail, location, contacts, photos or files. No third-party analytics or audience measurement is built into the game.

4. Rewarded ads (optional)

From version 1.0 the game may offer to watch a video ad in exchange for a reward. It is always your choice: never forced, never interstitial, at most 5 ads per day. Before the first ad, a consent prompt lets you accept or refuse use of the advertising ID. Without consent, no personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Recipients

  • A technical hosting provider running the game's server (processor).
  • Google (Google Play, Play Billing) for Android purchases, and Google AdMob for rewarded ads. They act under their own privacy policies.
  • Apple (App Store, TestFlight) for iOS distribution.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Google may process data outside the European Union (notably in the United States), relying on applicable adequacy decisions (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or Google's standard contractual clauses. We make no other voluntary transfer outside the EU.

7. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time.

  • Export: in the game, Settings > Account > Export my data.
  • Deletion: in the game, Settings > Account > Delete, or by e-mail: see Account deletion.
  • Other requests: lufygate@gmail.com. We reply within one month.
  • You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority, in France the CNIL.

8. Children

Cryptorche is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Encrypted communications (HTTPS), time-limited tokens, credentials encrypted on the device, logs without IP addresses or request contents.

10. Changes

The update date is shown at the top. Any significant change will be announced in the game.
