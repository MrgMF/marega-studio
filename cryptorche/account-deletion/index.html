Cryptorche: account deletion

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Version française

This page applies to the app Cryptorche, published by Marega Studio. The account is anonymous: it has no name or e-mail.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Cryptorche.
  2. Go to Settings > Account.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

The account is deleted on the server first, then local data is erased.

Option 2: by e-mail (without reinstalling the game)

Write to lufygate@gmail.com with the subject "Cryptorche: account deletion" and include your account's 20-character recovery code (Settings > Account) or your anonymous account identifier. Without one of these we cannot identify your account. We delete the account and confirm by reply within 30 days at most.

What is deleted

The account and its identifier, balances (shards, gems), quests, season pass, inventory and the match summaries attached to it.

What is kept

The transaction ledger is kept in anonymised form, unlinked from the deleted account, to meet accounting and legal obligations. Purchases made through Google Play remain available from Google under its own rules. Data already sent to Google AdMob is governed by Google's policy.

See also the privacy policy.
