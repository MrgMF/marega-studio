Bras de Fer: privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Version française

In short: the app works first on your device, in an encrypted database. Syncing with the Marega Studio server is optional, off by default and subject to your explicit consent. No advertising, no trackers, no analytics, no AI feature.

1. Data controller

Marega Studio (Fodié Marega, business registration in progress). Contact: lufygate@gmail.com. Marega Studio runs the app's account and sync server, hosted on Microsoft Azure in the European Union.

2. Data processed

  • What you enter: profile (goal, height, weight, age), workouts, loads, reps, meals, body measurements, habits, exercise photos, saved gyms. They are stored on your device in an encrypted database (SQLCipher) whose key stays in the device's secure keychain (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore). This data can reveal health information: it is sensitive data (special category, GDPR article 9).
  • Account: a random account identifier (a guest account created automatically, with no data in it). If you sign in with Apple or Google, the server keeps the opaque identifier they provide; neither your name nor your e-mail is requested or kept.
  • Location (optional): used only while the app is open, to recognise your gym; gym coordinates stay on the device unless you tick the option to include them in sync. No background location.
  • Camera: only to scan a barcode; the image is neither saved nor sent. Notifications: local reminders you turn on. No microphone, no access to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Never synced: photos, location.

3. Optional sync (health data)

Sync is off by default: without consent the app works locally only and no health data is sent. It is only offered once Marega Studio has opened the service. If you turn it on, after a dedicated screen with an unticked box and the exact purpose (backup and restore), your workouts, program, profile, meal history, measurements and a few settings are sent to the server over an encrypted connection, then resynced after each change and when the app opens.

  • Single purpose: giving your data back to you (reinstall, new phone). No analysis, no profiling, no advertising, no sharing with any third party.
  • Legal basis: your explicit consent (art. 6.1.a and 9.2.a). The server records the version of the text accepted, the date and the source, and refuses any sync read or write without valid consent.
  • Withdrawal as easy as giving: Settings > Account and sync > Withdraw my consent. Sync stops and all your synced data is erased from the server (the account is kept).
  • Encryption: content encrypted field by field (AES-256-GCM) before storage, with a key kept outside the database and backups; server disk encrypted at rest by Azure; encrypted backups. Technical metadata (collection, record identifier, version, dates, size) stays in clear.

5. Recipients, processors and transfers

  • Microsoft Azure (processor), region Denmark East, European Union: hosting of the server, its database and encrypted backups. An encrypted copy is sent to Azure storage in France (European Union). Your health data does not leave the European Union.
  • Apple or Google if you sign in with them: they know you use the app, never your data; they act under their own policy. Apple (App Store) or Google Play for the possible in-app purchase.
  • Open Food Facts (open database), contacted directly from your device when you search a food or scan a barcode: the text or number searched is sent, and your IP address is visible to that service. No other personal data.
  • Earlier versions offered an optional backup to your own private GitHub repository. It is no longer used; if you had set it up, old backups remain at GitHub and are not erased by the app: delete them yourself from your repository.
  • Stripe is not used. No advertising, no analytics.

The server makes no voluntary transfer outside the European Union. Apple, Google and Open Food Facts apply their own policies and transfer safeguards.

6. Backups

The server is backed up every night in encrypted form, with a copy before each deployment. On the server: 7 daily and 4 weekly backups (about 28 days). The copy hosted in France is kept for at most 90 days, plus 30 days of soft deletion. Data you erased may therefore remain, encrypted, in backups until these periods expire; after any restore, erasures already requested are replayed before the service is put back online.

7. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent (GDPR, articles 15 to 21 and 7.3).

  • Rectification, erasure, withdrawal of consent: directly in the app.
  • Erase your data: Settings > My data > Delete my data (device and server). Delete your account: Settings > Account and sync > Delete my account; the account, its synced data and its Apple or Google links are deleted, then the device is erased.
  • Access and full export: the app has no server export button. Write to lufygate@gmail.com stating your account identifier (Settings > Account and sync): we send you a JSON file of all server data attached to your account, decrypted, through an encrypted channel, within one month (extendable by two months for a complex request, with prior notice). We may ask for an additional verification element, without collecting more data than necessary.
  • You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr/fr/plaintes).

8. Age: digital majority

The app is for people aged 15 or over (age of digital majority in France, Data Protection Act, article 45), confirmed by a declaration. It is not intended for younger people; if you believe someone under 15 created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Disclaimer

Bras de Fer is a fitness log, not a medical device; it does not replace the advice of a health professional.
