Bras de Fer: privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Version française

In short: your data stays on your device, encrypted. Marega Studio runs no server for this app, shows no ads and uses no trackers or analytics.

1. Controller

Marega Studio (Fodié Marega, registration in progress). Contact: lufygate@gmail.com. The studio does not receive your data; you stay in control of where it is stored.

2. Data processed on your device

  • Fitness and health data you enter: workouts, exercises, weights lifted, body weight, measurements, progress.
  • Nutrition: foods, meals, goals.
  • App settings and, if you enable it, biometric lock (verification is done by your device's operating system; the app does not receive your biometric data).

This data is stored locally in an encrypted database (SQLCipher). The encryption key is kept in the device's secure keystore (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore) and never leaves it. Health data is sensitive: it is processed only to run the app for you, on the basis of your consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), given by entering it, and you can delete it at any time.

3. Network connections

  • GitHub backup (optional): if you set it up, the app uploads a backup of your data to your own private GitHub repository, using your access token, stored in the secure keystore. It refuses to write to a public repository. This transfer is governed by GitHub's privacy policy (hosting notably in the United States). The studio has no access to your token or repository. Without setup, nothing is sent.
  • Barcode food lookup: when you scan a product, the barcode is sent to the open Open Food Facts database to fetch nutrition information. No other personal data is sent, but your IP address is visible to that service.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan a barcode. Images are neither stored nor transmitted.
  • Notifications: reminders you enable.
  • No location, contacts or microphone permission (the microphone is explicitly blocked).

5. What we do not do

No ads, no trackers, no cookies, no audience analytics, no sharing or sale of data, no account with us.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app erases it. A GitHub backup is under your control: delete it from your repository.

7. Your rights

Since we do not hold your data, you exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability directly in the app and in your repository. Questions: lufygate@gmail.com (reply within one month). You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority, in France the CNIL.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

9. Disclaimer

Bras de Fer is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a health professional.
