Zorvo: privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Version française

Zorvo is a free mobile game (Android and iPhone) with optional in-app purchases, published by Marega Studio.

1. Data controller

Marega Studio (Fodié Marega, business registration in progress). Contact for any question or request: lufygate@gmail.com.

2. In short

  • No name, e-mail or phone number is requested. The game automatically creates a guest account (random identifier) for the shop, quests and saving your progress.
  • Your progress and purchases are stored on the Marega Studio server, hosted by Microsoft Azure in Denmark (European Union). The device keeps a local copy.
  • Signing in with Apple or Google is optional: if you choose it, the server keeps only the opaque identifier provided by Apple or Google, never your e-mail, name or photo.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no advertising identifier. The current version contains no advertising; if an optional rewarded ad is ever added, it will only be shown with your consent and this policy will be updated first.
  • The game does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store: Marega Studio never receives your card number.

4. Recipients and processors

  • Microsoft Azure (processor), region Denmark East (European Union): hosting of the game server, its database (SQLite) and encrypted backups. The disk is encrypted at rest by Azure.
  • Google: Google Play and Play Billing (distribution and payment of Android purchases; purchase verification: purchase token, product, order number and masked account identifier); Google Sign-In if you choose that sign-in method. Google acts for its own account under its own policy.
  • Apple: App Store (distribution and payment of iPhone purchases); Sign in with Apple if you choose that sign-in method. Apple acts for its own account under its own policy.
  • We do not use Stripe for Zorvo: the game is sold only through Google Play and the App Store. No advertising or analytics service is integrated.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

The game server, its database and its backups are hosted in the European Union (Denmark; backup copy in France) and no replication outside the Union is active. Google LLC and Apple Inc. are based in the United States: their processing relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, as set out in their own documents.

6. Backups

The server database is backed up every night in encrypted form, and a copy is made before each deployment. On the server we keep 7 daily and 4 weekly backups (about 28 days). An encrypted copy is sent daily to Azure storage in France (European Union), where it is kept for at most 90 days, plus 30 days of soft deletion. A deleted account may therefore remain in backups until these periods expire; after any restore, deletions already requested are replayed before the service is put back online.

7. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (GDPR, articles 15 to 21), and the right to withdraw consent.

  • Access and full export: the game has no export button. Write to lufygate@gmail.com: we send you a structured JSON file of all data attached to your account (account, progress, balances, ledger, inventory, matches, quests, pass, offers, chests, purchases, audit log entries concerning you) through an encrypted channel, within one month (extendable by two months for a complex request, with prior notice).
  • Erasure: from the game, or without the app; see Account deletion.
  • Objection to personalised offers and to anti-cheat processing: by e-mail; for anti-cheat it is examined case by case (protecting the game economy may take precedence).
  • As the account holds no name or e-mail, we may ask for an element proving the account is yours (for example the account identifier shown in settings), without collecting more data than necessary.
  • You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr/fr/plaintes) or your country's data protection authority.

8. Minors

Zorvo is for people aged 13 and over; we knowingly collect no data from children under 13. In France, between 13 and 15, a legal guardian's consent is required to use an online service and for purchases; a monthly spending cap can be set in the game. A parent who believes their child created an account can ask for its deletion at lufygate@gmail.com.

9. Security

Encrypted communications (HTTPS), short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh tokens, device secrets stored hashed on the server, rewards computed by the server, rate limiting, encrypted backups, server disk encrypted at rest, logs without IP addresses or request content. No system is infallible: do not share your recovery code, if any.

10. Changes

The update date is shown at the top of the page. Adding a feature that processes new data (for example advertising) will be announced in the game and, where the law requires it, subject to your consent.
