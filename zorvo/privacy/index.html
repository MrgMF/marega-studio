Zorvo: privacy policy

Last updated : 24 November 2026

Mobile game (Android and iPhone) published by Marega Studio. Early-access version.

1. Publisher and data controller

Zorvo is a free mobile game with in-app purchases, published by Fodié Marega, sole trader (entrepreneur individuel) trading as Marega Studio, the data controller. Contact for any question or request about personal data: lufygate@gmail.com. Postal address provided on request at the contact address.

2. Summary

  • No name, phone number or e-mail address is requested or stored. The game automatically creates a guest account (a random identifier) for the shop, quests and the backup of your progress.
  • You can play offline: a run does not depend on the network. Your progress (level, talents, outfits, statistics, streak) and purchases are saved on the studio server, hosted by Microsoft Azure in Denmark (European Union), and resynchronised as soon as the network returns; the device keeps a local copy.
  • Signing in with Apple or Google is coming soon and optional: it will let you recover this account after a reinstall. It is not active yet; once it is, the server will keep only the identifier provided by Apple or Google, never your e-mail address.
  • The game contains no advertising, no audience measurement and no trackers. It does not use the advertising ID.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Its permissions are limited to Internet, network state, vibration and Google Play billing (Android).
  • Payments are handled by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone): Marega Studio never receives your card number.
  • The game has no export button: to get your data, write to lufygate@gmail.com. You can delete your account from the game, or from a web page without having the app.

Personalized in-game offers

Data used: account age, activity (matches, active days, level, season-pass progress, recent results), inactivity and returning after an absence, purchase history and amounts, spending cap, offer and display history.

Logic: the server applies automatic rules and a random draw to select a content bonus at the same store price, based on progress, purchases or returning after an absence. It limits frequency and pauses offers after certain recent losses, substantial spending or ignored offers. This commercial personalization has no legal or similarly significant effect.

Purpose: present relevant commercial offers in the game store and measure their displays; no external advertising or sale of this data. Legal basis: the studio’s legitimate interest in promoting its content to its players (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), with a right to object at any time.

Retention: closed offers and events (display, dismissal, purchase) are retained for at most 13 months (396 days), or until account deletion if earlier. Source data follows the retention periods in the table above; the preference is kept until changed or the account is deleted.

Enabled by default: personalized offers start enabled. To opt out, open Settings → Account and data → “PERSONALIZED OFFERS: YES” and switch to “NO”. The server cancels the active offer and stops creating new ones. You can also object at lufygate@gmail.com. Opting out is free and does not prevent you from playing.

4. Recipients and processors

  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC): Google Play Billing and purchase verification (Google Play Developer interface). Google acts on its own behalf for payment and the purchase history of your Google account.
  • Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd / Apple Inc.): distribution through the App Store and payment of purchases on iPhone. Apple acts on its own behalf; verification of these purchases by our server will be enabled with iOS purchase support, and this policy will then be updated if necessary.
  • Microsoft Azure (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd / Microsoft Corporation; Denmark East region, within the European Union): hosting of the game server, its database and its encrypted backups.

We do not sell or share your data for advertising purposes.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Google LLC and Apple Inc. are established in the United States. These companies state that it adheres to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (European Commission adequacy decision of 10 July 2023) and additionally provide standard contractual clauses. The game server, its database and its backups are hosted in Denmark (European Union); no replication outside the European Union is active.

6. Backups

The server is backed up daily in encrypted form; copies are kept for 7 days (daily) and 4 weeks (weekly), then destroyed. A deleted item therefore disappears from backups by the end of this cycle at the latest.

7. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (GDPR Articles 15 to 21). The game has no export button: to get a copy of your data (account, progress, balances, currency history, inventory, runs, quests, pass, purchases), to have it rectified, or for any other request, write to lufygate@gmail.com; we reply within one month. The "Account and data" screen lets you delete your account yourself. As the account holds no name or e-mail, we may ask for something that lets us check that it is yours (for example its identifier, shown in the "Account and data" screen).

You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your own data protection authority.

8. Deleting your account and data

  1. In the game: "Account and data" screen, "Delete my account" (double confirmation). The account, tokens, saved progress, inventory, runs, quests and pass are erased from the server; currencies lose their owner; the device forgets its online identity.
  2. Without the app: see the Account deletion page.
  3. On the device: local progression remains playable offline; uninstall the game or clear its data in the device settings to remove it.
  4. Purchases: your purchase history and refunds remain managed by Google Play or the App Store; deleting the game account does not erase what Google or Apple keeps, and does not refund purchases.

9. Security

Exchanges with the server must use HTTPS. The account identity is stored in an encrypted file on the device; the encryption key is protected by the iOS Keychain (accessible after first unlock, bound to this device) or Android Keystore (non-exportable AES/GCM key). Mobile builds refuse file-based key storage; Android backups and data transfers are disabled. A compromised device may still expose data while it is in use. Access tokens are short-lived, renewal tokens rotate at each use (a replayed token revokes the session), request rates are limited, rewards are computed by the server and not by the game, and backups are encrypted. As no system is infallible, do not share your recovery code.

10. Minors

Zorvo is intended for people aged 13 and over; we knowingly collect no data about children under 13. In France, between 13 and 15, the agreement of a legal guardian is required: a minor under 15 needs the agreement of a legal guardian to use an online service, notably to make purchases; you can set a monthly spending limit in the game. A parent who believes their child created an account can ask for its deletion at lufygate@gmail.com.

11. Changes

Any change will be published on this page with its date. Adding a new feature that processes data (for example advertising) will be announced in the game and, where the law requires it, subject to your consent before it is enabled.
