Zorvo: account deletion

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Version française

This page applies to the game Zorvo, published by Marega Studio. The account is a guest account: it has no name or e-mail (except an opaque Apple or Google identifier if you linked one).

Option 1: in the game (immediate)

  1. Open Zorvo.
  2. Go to the "Account and data" screen.
  3. Choose "Delete my account" and confirm (a fresh sign-in and a single-use confirmation, valid for 5 minutes, are required).

The account is deleted on the server, then the device forgets its online identity. Local progress remains playable offline until you uninstall the game or clear its data in the device settings.

Option 2: by e-mail (without reinstalling the game)

Write to lufygate@gmail.com with the subject "Zorvo account deletion" and give the account identifier shown in the game. If you can no longer access the game, give any element that helps find your account (for example the approximate creation date or a Google Play order reference). We may ask for a verification element. We handle the request and confirm within one month at most.

What is deleted

The account and its identifier, sign-in tokens, the Apple or Google link, saved progress, currencies and inventory, matches, quests, season pass, offers and chests attached to it.

What is kept

  • The currency transaction ledger, for 10 years, with no link to you (accounting obligation).
  • The Google Play purchase token and order number: 13 months after deletion (anti-replay, refunds, bank disputes), then deleted.
  • Encrypted server backups: the account may remain in them for up to 28 days on the server and up to 90 days (plus 30 days of soft deletion) on the copy hosted in France; deletions are replayed after any restore.
  • At Google or Apple: the purchase history of your Google or Apple account stays managed by them. Deleting the game account does not refund purchases.
  • E-mails exchanged with us: 3 years after the last exchange.

An empty guest account (no data and no purchase) that is inactive for more than 30 days is deleted automatically.
